# Amescom website decision record

Use this record to turn the questionnaire into concrete implementation inputs. The entries below are proposals, not approved decisions. Add the decision owner and date when accepting or changing them.

## Working assumptions

- **D01 — Purpose:** public marketing website with “Contact us” as the primary conversion journey. Status: approved by owner. Linked questions: Q01, Q09.
- **D02 — Language:** Arabic first; content structure ready for English. Status: proposed. Linked question: Q03.
- **D03 — Main action:** “Contact us,” linking to `/ar/contact` and the contact form. Product/sector context may be carried into the form. Status: approved by owner. Linked questions: Q05, Q26.
- **D04 — Products:** prepare four draft product pages; FRONA publication is held pending confirmation of its current public name and availability. All products and company content must be presented under Amescom only. Status: partially approved. Linked questions: Q02, Q04, Q18.
- **D05 — Visual direction:** spacious light design inspired by Notion's hierarchy, with Amescom branding and proposed blue accent. Status: proposed. Linked question: Q06.
- **D06 — Content editing:** structured local content initially; CMS only if editing needs justify it. Status: proposed. Linked question: Q33.
- **D07 — Stack:** reuse suitable existing code; for a new project consider Next.js/TypeScript after hosting review. Status: proposed. Linked question: Q08.
- **D08 — Integrations:** no provider chosen. Form destinations, email delivery, storage and scanning are unresolved production dependencies. Linked questions: Q09, Q27, Q28, Q30.
- **D09 — Publication claims:** omit unsupported quantitative/absolute claims and unverified certification language. Status: proposed. Linked questions: Q07, Q13, Q15.
- **D10 — Tracking:** no optional tracking or newsletter by default. Status: proposed. Linked question: Q32.
- **D11 — Recruitment:** minimal application fields; no date of birth without a documented need. Status: proposed. Linked question: Q29.
