# Homepage extension — 2026-11-04

The owner approved the ARCHIVE prototype and requested the homepage next, before other product-page redesigns.

## Direction contract (iteration 1 of 4)

THESIS: Lead with cinematic photography, not an interactive product mock. The first viewport is a full-bleed software scene with a large Arabic headline; products are told through crossfading images. This refuses the previous tabbed UI sandbox.

OWN-WORLD: Dark photographic hero, white editorial body, Amescom blue pills, El Messiri display, Noto Sans Arabic body, large rounded image wells.

STORY: See Amescom as institutional software, browse four products through images, then contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed rotating tech photographs, right-aligned (RTL) display headline, contact and product actions on the image. Product stage sits in the next section.

FORM: Visual reference https://we4u.app/de/ for layout, image scale and motion. Amescom identity, Arabic RTL, brochure JPGs plus generated tech photographs. Seed: none (owner-pinned reference).

FINISH: Iteration 1 stops after desktop and mobile review of the homepage only. Later iterations wait for owner feedback.
