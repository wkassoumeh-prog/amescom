# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: a business or department leader in a Syrian or regional institution who needs to understand Amescom’s software and contact the company. Secondary: an IT, security or procurement reviewer checking product fit; an existing customer looking for help; a job applicant checking careers.

## Product Purpose

Amescom’s public website is a marketing site. It explains the company, its products and sectors, and converts visitors to a contact enquiry. Product capabilities described in brochures are content, not software to build on this site.

Success: a visitor can identify a suitable product and reach `/ar/contact` without dead ends, invented proof, or non-Amescom branding.

## Positioning

Amescom is the only company identity on the site. It sells institutional software (document archive, workflow, clinical operations, operational risk) plus related services (infrastructure, security, networks), with Arabic-first presentation and Damascus contact details.

## Operating Context

Arabic RTL public pages. Preview/content modes control what is published. Forms may run in local mock mode until a live destination is approved. FRONA is included in the product lineup and navigation at the owner's explicit request (2026-11-04).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- In scope: company pages, product and sector pages, contact, help, careers, privacy (when approved), metadata.
- Out of scope: patient portal, medical records, risk platform, archive engine, workflow builder, payments, customer login.
- Do not invent team biographies, statistics, client logos, partner badges, street addresses, or unapproved claims.
- The owner authorizes using photographs and illustrations from the supplied brochures. Extract clean image resources where possible. Label illustrative demonstrations accurately and omit all non-Amescom company branding.
- Founding year 2004, Facebook, and several technical claims remain unapproved for public use.

## Brand Commitments

- Public names: أميسكوم / Amescom. Product names stay Latin: AMESCOM ARCHIVE, AMESCOM Workflow, AMESCOM Clinical PRO, FRONA.
- Visual reference for layout, typography, product storytelling and motion (latest owner request): https://www.notion.com/. Retain Amescom identity and Arabic RTL content.
- Current approval scope: the owner approved the ARCHIVE page and requested the homepage next (2026-11-04). Extend its visual language to the homepage. Redesign other product pages only when separately authorized.
- Temporary text wordmark until an original logo is supplied.
- No third-party company branding in assets.

## Evidence on Hand

Structured Arabic content in `content/amescom/`. Original photographic illustrations in `public/media/` (generated for this site; not Amescom premises, staff or product UI). Original process diagrams in `public/diagrams/` (draw.io sources plus SVG). Brochure image resources may be used in the ARCHIVE prototype as explicitly requested by the owner.

## Product Principles

- Contact us is the primary action; explore products is secondary.
- Show the work with strong photography, brochure imagery and clear interactive demonstrations. Keep illustrations distinct from actual software screenshots.
- Keep claims sourced and omit anything unapproved.
- Arabic reading, RTL layout and accessibility come before visual flourish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Visible focus, keyboard menus, skip link, and no clipping of Arabic.
